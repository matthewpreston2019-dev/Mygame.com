Install on iPhone

  1. Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari (top on iPad).
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. Then open Companion from your Home Screen — notifications only work from there.

Needs iOS 16.4 or newer for notifications.
