HOW TO PLAY

Objective · Rift Control

Stand in the glowing Rift with no enemies inside to fill your team's meter. First team to 100% wins. A knockout adds +3%. The Rift shifts location every 40 seconds. After 3 minutes the higher meter wins.

Breaking rules

Every hero can rewrite one law of the arena with their RULE ability (E or right mouse). Landing hits charges it faster.

The arena fights back

Chrono Pylons — shoot one to freeze time around it: everyone and every bullet inside stops.
Launch Pads — step on to vault across the map.
Data Crates — breakable cover.
